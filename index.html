Inside the app

Five tabs, one clear picture

Every screen is built around a single idea: you should be able to see your financial life at a glance without giving anyone access to it.

◆

Dashboard

Net worth, total assets, liabilities, month-to-date income and expense, savings rate and current portfolio value — with a custom-drawn six-month cash flow chart and an interactive category donut. Tap any segment to focus it.

≡

Activity

A real ledger. Income, expense and transfer entries across any account, filterable by time range, account or category, searchable by note, with receipt photos attached from the camera or photo library.

◑

Budgets

Set a monthly, weekly or yearly cap per category and watch your pace in real time. Progress, remaining room and over-budget categories stay visible at a glance.

◔

Portfolio

Stocks, ETFs, funds, bonds, crypto and real estate. Quantity, average cost and a current price that you maintain yourself, with cost basis and unrealized P/L computed from your own entries.

▤

Reports

Income, expense and balance trends over the periods you care about, broken down by account and category so patterns become obvious instead of buried.

⚙

Settings

Base currency across 14 currencies, dark theme, a Hide Amounts switch that masks every number on screen, and a one-tap reset that erases everything on the device.

What it is not

A journal, not a brokerage

XM Vaultfolio does not connect to banks, brokers, exchanges or wallets. It does not place, transmit or hold orders, and it never fetches live market quotes. It is a record of what you already know about your own money.

  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, no email required.
  • No cloud. Your records live in the app's private sandbox on this device.
  • No tracking. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no fingerprinting.
  • No advice. Every number shown is a reflection of what you typed, not a recommendation.
  • No custody. You cannot buy, sell, send or receive any asset through the app.

Privacy is the product, not a feature

XM Vaultfolio has no backend. Your transactions, holdings, budgets, notes and receipt photos are written to the app's local sandbox and never leave the device. Uninstall the app and the data is gone — unless you exported it yourself. See the full Privacy Policy.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does XM Vaultfolio connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank connection, no Open Banking integration and no account aggregation. Every record is entered by you, by hand.

Where are my prices coming from?

From you. Current prices are values you maintain yourself. The app never requests live market data, so a valuation shown in the app is your personal record and may differ from a real-time quote by minutes, hours or days.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Each device keeps its own independent copy. There is no sync and no cloud backup — if you move to a new phone, export your data before you remove the app.

Is XM Vaultfolio financial advice?

No. It is an organizational and analytical tool. It does not provide investment, tax or legal advice, and it makes no recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything.

How do I delete my data?

Settings offers a Reset All Data action that erases every transaction, account, holding, budget and receipt on the device, and restores the built-in categories. Uninstalling the app removes the sandbox as well.
