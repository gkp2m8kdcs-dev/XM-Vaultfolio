Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what XM Vaultfolio ("the App", "we", "us") does with information when you use it on your iPhone or iPad.

Effective date: 22 October 2026
The short version

XM Vaultfolio has no servers and no user accounts. Everything you enter is written to your device's local storage and stays there. We do not collect personal data, we do not run analytics or advertising, and we cannot see your information at all.

1. Who we are

XM Vaultfolio is a personal wealth journal app for iOS. It is designed to let individuals record and organise their own accounts, transactions, budgets and portfolio positions. It is not a brokerage, custodian, exchange, bank, or investment adviser, and it does not connect to any of them.

Because the App operates entirely offline on your device, "we" have no access to the personal financial information you record in it.

2. Data stored entirely on your device

The App stores the following information locally, in the private sandbox container assigned to the App by iOS:

  • Financial records you create — accounts and account types, transaction entries (amount, date, category, account, note), budgets and category caps, holdings with quantity, average cost and current price, and any notes you attach.
  • Images you choose to attach — receipt or statement photographs captured with the camera or picked from your photo library.
  • App preferences — base currency, theme choice, the Hide Amounts setting, monthly start day, and other configuration values.

This information is used by the App on your device to generate the dashboard totals, charts, budget progress, reports and portfolio calculations you see. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no account creation step, no sign-in, and no cloud synchronization.

Each device maintains its own independent copy of your data. If you install the App on a second device, that device starts empty.

3. Information we never collect

The App does not collect, request, upload, sell or share any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any other contact information.
  • Any account, password, credential, or login for a bank, broker, exchange, or wallet.
  • Any payment method, card number, or billing information.
  • Your location, including precise and approximate location.
  • Your contacts, your full photo library, or any file you have not explicitly selected.
  • Your device identifier, advertising identifier (IDFA), or usage, funnel, or crash analytics.
  • Any financial account details obtained from a third-party institution.
  • Any content you create, including your financial records, notes, and photographs.

4. Device permissions the App may request

The App performs no background network activity. It may request the following permissions, and only at the moment you initiate the related action. Declining any permission does not limit any other feature of the App.

Camera

Requested when you choose to photograph a receipt or statement in order to attach it to a transaction or holding. Images captured this way are stored locally by the App.

Photo library (read)

Requested when you choose to select an existing image to attach as a receipt or note. The App accesses only the images you select and does not browse, index, or otherwise read the remainder of your library.

Photo library (add)

Requested only when you choose to save or share a report image you generated inside the App. The App never writes to your library unless you ask it to.

You can change these permissions at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security on your device. Revoking a permission prevents the related action from working; the rest of the App continues to function normally.

5. Storage location and protection

Your records are written to the App's sandboxed container directory on your device using standard iOS file protection. When your device is locked, this storage is protected in accordance with the security settings configured on the device itself. Because your data never travels over a network, it is not exposed to the transmission risks that cloud-hosted applications face.

Please observe the following limitations, which are outside our control:

  • A jailbroken or otherwise compromised device can undermine any local data protection.
  • Device backups created through iCloud or a computer backup may include App data. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy practices and your own Apple device settings.
  • If you take a screenshot of the App and save it to your photo library, that image leaves the App's control and is retained according to your own storage settings.

6. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, trade, or license your data, because we do not possess it. No data from the App is disclosed to advertising networks, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

We would disclose information only where legally required, and in practice no such disclosure is possible because the information resides solely on your device. If we are ever legally compelled to respond to a valid order regarding App-held data, we will notify you to the extent permitted by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained on your device until you delete it. You control deletion in three ways:

  • Delete individual items — remove any account, transaction, budget, or holding from within the App.
  • Reset all data — the Settings screen provides a "Reset All Data" action that erases every transaction, account, holding, budget, and receipt image stored by the App, and restores the built-in categories. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstall the App — removing the App from your device deletes its sandbox container and all data it contains.

We do not hold a backup of your data anywhere, so we cannot recover it for you. If you are leaving a device behind, reset the data before uninstalling.

8. Third-party services and links

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, or social SDKs. It makes no network requests to any server operated by us or by anyone else.

If the App displays a link to an external website, that website is governed by its own privacy policy, which we neither control nor endorse. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites.

9. Children

XM Vaultfolio is a general-audience financial organization tool and is not directed at children under 13 or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction. Because the App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, no child data is gathered, stored, or transmitted.

10. Security

Because all data remains on your device, the primary security control is the device itself. We recommend that you enable a strong device passcode and use Face ID or Touch ID, and that you keep your device's operating system and installed apps up to date. If your device is lost or stolen, the App's storage inherits the encryption and lock-state protections of iOS.

11. Your rights and choices

You have full control over the information the App stores:

  • View, edit, export, or delete any record at any time, directly on your device.
  • Mask every monetary figure on screen using the Hide Amounts setting.
  • Erase the complete dataset whenever you choose.
  • Remove the App and all of its data by uninstalling it.

Because we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on your behalf, and no data-processing agreement or consent mechanism is required.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect a new feature or to clarify existing wording. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and the App Store listing will reflect the current version. Material changes will be announced through the App's release notes.

Your continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
